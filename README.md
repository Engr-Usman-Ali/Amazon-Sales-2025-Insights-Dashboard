# Amazon Sales Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Introduction
This project analyzes Amazon’s sales performance using an interactive Power BI dashboard.  
The goal is to understand product performance, customer behavior, regional sales distribution, and overall order status.


## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
Before building the dashboard, several cleaning steps were performed:

### ✔ Handling Missing Data
- Checked for null values and fixed incomplete entries.
- Removed rows with invalid order status.

### ✔ Standardization
- Unified date formats.
- Cleaned customer names (removed extra spaces).
- Standardized product names.

### ✔ Outlier Review
- Verified unusually high or low sales values.
- Cleaned inconsistent canceled-order entries.

### ✔ Categorization
Orders were organized into:
- Completed Orders  
- Pending Orders  
- Canceled Orders

## 🔍 Key Insights

### **1. Sales Performance**
- Completed sales amount totals **88.53K**.
- Pending and canceled orders combined make up more than 60%, showing operational gaps.

### **2. Monthly Trends**
- Sales rose from Month 2 to 3 but dropped significantly in Month 4.
- This indicates seasonal variation or supply issues.

### **3. Top 5 Products**
1. Laptop  
2. Refrigerator  
3. Smartphone  
4. Smartwatch  
5. Washing Machine  

High-value electronics drive the most revenue.

### **4. Customer Insights**
- Customers like **Chris White**, **Daniel Harris**, and **David Lee** contribute strong sales.
- Some customers show high cancellation behavior, indicating issues in checkout or delivery.

### **5. Region-wise Sales**
Top performing regions:
- Miami  
- Houston  
- Seattle  

Low performing:
- Boston  
- Denver  
- Chicago  



## ⚠ Problems Identified
- High cancellations (77 orders).  
- Large count of pending orders (85).  
- Drop in Month 4 sales.  
- Weak presence in some cities.  
- Customer dissatisfaction indicators (repeated cancellations).  


## 💡 Recommendations

### **Operational Improvements**
- Improve order processing to reduce pending orders.
- Investigate cancellation reasons (stock-out, pricing, Deliver delays).

### **Marketing & Sales**
- Focus campaigns in weak regions: Boston, Chicago, Denver.
- Offer promotions for low-selling products.

### **Customer Retention**
- Provide loyalty rewards to top customers.
- Contact frequent cancellation customers for feedback.

### **Product Strategy**
- Prioritize high-demand products (Laptop, Refrigerator, Smartphone).
- Ensure better restocking of fast-moving items.



## 🏁 Conclusion
The dashboard highlights strong sales performance but also reveals operational and regional gaps.  
Improving order fulfillment, reducing cancellations, and strengthening low-performing regions will boost growth and customer satisfaction.
